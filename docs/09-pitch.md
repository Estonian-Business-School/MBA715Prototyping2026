# 09 — Pitch

**Due:** outline with Checkpoint 4 (4 Dec) · deck draft 9 Dec · final 11 Dec
**Format at defense:** 12 minutes pitch and live demo, 6 minutes Q&A

## Narrative outline
| Part | Content | Time |
|---|---|---|
| 1. The shift | What has changed in the customer's world that makes this problem urgent now | 1 min |
| 2. The stakes | What the customer loses today (time, money, risk) — with numbers from your research | 1.5 min |
| 3. The promised land | What their work looks like with your product | 1 min |
| 4. Live demo | The core workflow, role by role | 4 min |
| 5. Evidence | Interviews, willingness to pay, test results V1 → V2 | 2 min |
| 6. Business model | Who pays, how much, path to first customers | 1.5 min |
| 7. The ask | What you need for the next microdegree (sign-off, development help) | 1 min |
